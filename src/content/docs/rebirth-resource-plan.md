### Historical archive hints

- Old DanceNet archive and FTP song collections were referenced in 2009 and are often mirrored on Archive.org or vintage synth sites.
- ReBirth Museum previously hosted official song downloads; the original site now redirects to Reason Studios, but many archives are preserved via peff.com and other mirrors.

## 3. Unusual / Interesting Extras

- Tool for extracting full song arrangements, automation, and MIDI from `.rbs` files.
- Included in the Archive.org pack: https://archive.org/details/rebirth-2.01-2001-version (look for `Remaker 1.32.zip`).

## 4. Legal & compatibility notes

- Most `.rbm` mods and `.rbs` songs require ReBirth RB-338 v2.0.1.
- ReBirth v2.0.1 is legally available as freeware via Archive.org ISO/torrent mirrors; search terms like "ReBirth RB-338 2.0.1".
- Files are old; test compatibility in original Windows/Mac environments or via compatibility layers.
- Respect original copyrights and treat these as non-commercial nostalgia shares.
- Bulk downloads are often ZIP/ISO packs containing many tracks and mods.
